Jokers


There are a total of 150 unique jokers in Balatro so again, you may want to use text to speech
for this section. Jokers are the most common thing you can find in the shop and are found in
Buffoon packs or in packs given by the Buffoon skip tag. The Rare and Uncommon skip tags guarantee
a joker of that rarity in the shop. There are also legendary jokers that are only found by obtaining
The Soul which is rarely found in Arcana packs and Spectral packs. Here are the jokers sorted by rarity:
